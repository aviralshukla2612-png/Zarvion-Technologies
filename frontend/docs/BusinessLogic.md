# Business Logic

## What Zarvion Technologies Does

Zarvion Technologies is a **premium recruitment agency** that connects:
```
Candidates → Recruiters → Companies
```

It is NOT a job portal. Candidates do not apply to jobs directly. Instead, Zarvion provides premium career services and places candidates with partner companies.

## User Journeys

### Candidate Journey
1. Lands on Home → sees Hero (typing animation + 3D road)
2. Scrolls to About → learns about Zarvion
3. Scrolls to Services → sees 6 service cards (sticky stacking)
4. Clicks "Learn More" → goes to `/services/:slug` (ServiceDetails)
5. Clicks "Get Started" → goes to `/get-started` (onboarding form)
6. Submits form → success screen

### Role Exploration Journey
1. Scrolls to DemandedRoles → horizontal carousel of 7 role cards
2. Clicks a card → goes to `/roles/:slug` (RoleDetails)
3. Sees salary, demand, skills → clicks "Apply Now" (mailto link)

### Direct Contact Journey
1. Scrolls to Contact section (or navigates to `/contact`)
2. Hovers the right panel → form reveals
3. Fills form → submits (currently no backend)
